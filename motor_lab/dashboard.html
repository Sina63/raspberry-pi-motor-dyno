How this test works

Click New test with motor output OFF. Capture the 45-second baseline while turning the shaft slowly in the selected direction, then release it for the 10-second pause. Click Arm sweep, clear your hands and turn motor output ON at the selected voltage, with the supply current limit set to 2 A. The program switches 0 → selected maximum → 1 → 0. Loads 1–5 hold 5 s, load 6 holds 3 s, load 7 holds 2 s. Stay on this page while running. Turn motor power OFF when finished or aborted.

The software opens K8 if measured current reaches 1.9 A, RPM falls too far, data goes stale or the controlling browser heartbeat is lost. It cannot cut motor power, measure resistor temperature or protect against a hung Pi or welded contacts.
